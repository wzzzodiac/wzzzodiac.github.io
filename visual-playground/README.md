# Visual Playground

A collection of web design experiments: practice, hits, misses and happy accidents.

The catalogue lists exactly **Editorial, Personal Hub, Pastel Pink, Dimension, Multiverse and CASI**.
Cat studies keep their existing design/content. Their style selectors contain the same six active editions. Playful, Dark Retro and Ethereal are archived: omitted from selectors/catalogue, with their original assets and direct routes retained. Existing `/cats/` links and the Hub's `#cats` anchor remain compatible.

CASI is a publication copy of Atlas × Cauce Playground 005. Its illustration, font, motion and PNG export are embedded in its HTML. Warhol Arts / Serhii Polyvanyi / BL/S® is credited in its footer and credits dialog. Anton's full OFL is included there. The original local deliverable is unchanged.

Dimension and Multiverse retain their HTML5 UP / AJ credits and CC BY 3.0 licenses. The cat photography, fonts and archived reference editions retain their provenance in [`../cats/credits.html`](../cats/credits.html) and [`../cats/SOURCES.md`](../cats/SOURCES.md). Catalogue thumbnails are captures of these local editions, resized to WebP.
