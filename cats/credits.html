Reading the cat

Body-language notes and the random facts are short, general paraphrases of Cats Protection’s body-language guide. A signal is a clue, not a diagnosis. Read the whole cat in context.

The gallery personalities and “office hours” are playful captions, not behavioural classifications. Coat descriptions do not assign a breed to any photographed cat.
