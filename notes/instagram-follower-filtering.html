Instagram Unfollowers

See who does not follow you back and which followers disappeared since your previous scan. Read-only, in English, with 20 accounts per page.

Windows shortcut

DOWNLOAD WINDOWS ZIP ↓

Extract all the ZIP files, then double-click Open Instagram Unfollowers.bat. Instagram opens in your default browser and the script is copied for you. Log in, open Console, paste and press Enter, then click RUN.

Includes the launcher, the script and a short START HERE.txt. Keep all three files together. No installation needed.

Read-only / your signed-in account

This version cannot follow or unfollow anyone. It only reads follower lists after you press RUN, keeping pauses between requests. Whitelists and scan dates are saved in your browser, separately for each signed-in account. Instagram can change or limit these requests.

01

Open your Instagram profile

Log in normally and go to Instagram → Profile. The script is intended to be run while you are already inside Instagram with your own session.

02

Open the browser console

Press Ctrl+Shift+J, or right-click the page → Inspect → Console.

03

Paste the script and press Enter

If you used the Windows launcher, the code is already on your clipboard. Otherwise, open the code box below and click COPY CODE. In the Instagram console, press Ctrl+V, then Enter. Close any older panel before pasting.

04

Click RUN

Click RUN to scan. Not following back shows accounts you follow that do not follow you back. No longer following compares with your previous completed scan; your first scan saves the baseline. A missing follower may also be a deleted, deactivated or blocked account. Add accounts to your Whitelist to hide them from both lists.

Short version

  • Instagram → Profile.
  • Right-click → Inspect.
  • Open Console.
  • Paste the complete code.
  • Press Enter.
  • Click RUN.
  • Keep using the same browser profile for your whitelist and scan history.
  • Two complete scans are needed to show changes over time.
